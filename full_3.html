Stage 00 · Data Ingest & Per-Stock Thresholds

NIFTY 50 selector plus a 2-input OR gate: CSV history ∨ Fyers feed (live bar wins). The analyser computes VWAP, ATR₁₄, S, ΔS, σ₋, BS and V/V₂₀. Entry thresholds are now percentiles of the stock's previous ≤40 sessions (|S| 85th, ΔS 75th, σ₋ 10th), computed excluding today so nothing leaks from the future; the volume spike is a fixed 1.5×V₂₀. Select several CSVs at once (file name must contain the symbol) to enable the market-agreement filter.

Stage 01 · BO&FO Gate Bank (G01–G15)

Qualification = G01/G02 (distance from VWAP) ∧ G03/G04 (momentum) ∧ G06 (1.5× volume) ∧ G07 (ready), guarded by the failed-breakout memory (G10/G11, Sₙ < 0.70·Sₙ₋ⱼ) and the weak-break caution (G14). G05 (BS) is shown for information only — it restated G01. Only Priority Route 5 emits Q↑/Q↓.

Stage 02 · Trade Gate, Halts & Regime

6-input AND gate G: feed health, history ≥ 64 bars, missing bars ≤ 3, entry window 09:30–14:30 IST, not-choppy filter (VWAP crossed ≤ 3 times in 30 bars) and ATR guard. The spectral-entropy and SNR tests were removed: they almost never pass on stock returns. 4-input OR gate H still catches price/ATR faults, missing bars, Eq 92 flash shocks and Eq 94 restart pauses. The regime strip stays as a manual override.

Stage 03 · Eq 51 Directional Convergence

Joins Enable with Q↑/Q↓ and the market-agreement pills (≥ 60% of loaded stocks on the same side of their own VWAP; fewer than 5 aligned stocks → ?, which holds). The old score gate is gone: the score now only ranks candidates when several stocks qualify.

Stage 04 · Position DEMUX & Exit Priority

When FLAT, checks sizing (risk ≤ 0.5–1% of capital, 20% exposure cap). When OPEN: stop 1.2 ATR; sell half at +1 ATR and move the stop to entry + costs; rest at +2 ATR or a 1-ATR trail; time stop 12 bars or 15:15 IST. Same-bar stop ties beat targets. Max 2 new trades a day; stop after 2 losses.
